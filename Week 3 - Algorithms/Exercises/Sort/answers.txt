sort3 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know?: sort3 has almost the same runtime for a small dataset whether the input is sorted, reversed, or random. For 5,000 values, the runtimes are 0.043 seconds for sorted data, 0.045 seconds for reversed data, and 0.050 seconds for random data.
